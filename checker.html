Invoices are matched on supplier GSTIN plus invoice number. Spacing, case and leading zeros are ignored, so inv-3 and INV/0003 count as the same invoice.

This tool flags differences for review. It is not tax advice, and a qualified CA should check the results before any claim or filing. If your GSTR-2B has several sheets, the sheet with the most recognisable columns is used.
