Four kinds of problems, sorted for you

Amount mismatch

The invoice is in both files, but the tax is different. You see the exact difference in rupees.

Missing in GSTR-2B

You recorded it, the portal doesn't show it. The supplier may not have filed yet, and the credit may not be claimable.

Missing in books

The portal shows an invoice you never recorded. Either an entry was missed or it isn't yours.

Possible matches

Same supplier, same amounts, slightly different invoice number. Usually a typo, and easy to fix once you see it.

How it works

1

Upload two files

Your purchase register and your GSTR-2B, as Excel or CSV. Title rows, extra sheets and two-row headers are fine.

2

It matches invoice by invoice

Supplier GSTIN plus invoice number. Spacing, capitals and leading zeros are ignored, so inv-3 and INV/0003 count as the same invoice.

3

Download the report

One Excel file with a summary and a tab for each kind of problem, ready to send to a supplier or your CA.
